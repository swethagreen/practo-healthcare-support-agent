Consultation fees vary depending on the medical specialty, clinic location, and clinician experience. For this demonstration, General Medicine consultations cost ₹500, Cardiology consultations cost ₹1,200, Dermatology consultations cost ₹800, Pediatrics consultations cost ₹700, and Orthopedics consultations cost ₹1,000. Additional charges for diagnostic tests, procedures, or medications may apply and are not included in the consultation fee. Patients should verify the final amount displayed during booking before confirming an appointment.
